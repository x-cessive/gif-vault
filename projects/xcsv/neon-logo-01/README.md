# XCSV neon logo sting

First GIF of the XCSV brand program: a neon-gothic 8-bit "XCSV" sign
that buzzes and flickers like a real neon tube, with a crescent moon,
twinkling starfield, scanlines, and a bat crossing the top.

- Canvas: 160x100 pixel grid, NEAREST x2 to 320x200, 30 frames @ ~12fps,
  seamless loop, 32 KB.
- Rig: `~/workspace/xcsv-gifs/rig/neon_logo.py` (standalone PIL script,
  not the Gigi office rig — this is XCSV brand work, outside the Gigi spec).
- Giphy: tagged `xcessive`, `xcsv` — searchable as #xcsv on any platform
  with Giphy search. Upload results in `giphy_upload_results.json`.

- `xcsv_neon_logo` — the sting (flicker frames + bat flyover).
