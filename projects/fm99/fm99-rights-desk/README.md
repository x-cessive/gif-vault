# fm99 — fm99-rights-desk

The Now Spinning 99.9 FM rights desk: 30 states, one constant department room.
512x512, 96 frames @24fps, seamless loops. 8-bit pixel-art rig (128x128 grid,
NEAREST x4, solid colors, no AA), same recipe and QC gates as the standing
production standard.

Character: tortoise (APPROVED stamp). Room: legal office — filing cabinets, stamp pad, certificate frames.
Personality: slow, deliberate, unshakeable; comedy at tortoise speed.
